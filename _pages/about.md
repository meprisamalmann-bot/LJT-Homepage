I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Prof. Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
